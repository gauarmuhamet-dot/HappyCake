Contact Happy Cake

Happy Cake bakery welcomes customers who want to order fresh desserts for birthdays, family celebrations and special events. Our bakery provides different types of cakes with unique designs, flavours and decoration options. Customers can contact us to ask about available desserts, prices, delivery options and personalised cake orders.

The bakery is located in Astana, Kazakhstan. Visitors can come to our bakery during working hours to choose desserts and discuss their special requests. Our team helps customers select the best cake depending on the occasion, number of guests and personal preferences.
